# NAI-Redd

**Redd** it's an autonomous drone that fly with remote control phone app or you can just talk during the app with the AI to let him move as you want. I build it just because it's interesting idea to build your companion drone that follows you and talk to you.

## Design

It's used these components:
- XIAO ESP32-S3 Sense, MPU6050, VL53L3CX, BMP280, 4-in-1 ESC (Axisflying 30A), N-AI carrier board, 4S LiPo 1300 mAh, 4× EMAX ECO II 2004 3000KV, 3.5" 3-blade props.
